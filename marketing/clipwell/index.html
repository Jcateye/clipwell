FAQ

Questions before installing.

Does Clipwell upload my clipboard history?

No. Clipwell is designed to store clipboard history locally on your Mac.

Can I stop Clipwell from watching certain apps?

Yes. Use ignored apps for places where clipboard history should not be captured.

How does translation work?

Translation can use macOS Translation by default or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint when you configure one.

What can Clipwell preview?

Clipwell handles text, rich text, HTML, images, media, documents, and file URLs.
