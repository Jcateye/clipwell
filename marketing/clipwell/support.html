Clipwell Support

For launch support, contact MioMiao Labs LLC, operated by 深圳弥傲科技有限公司, through the support email you choose for App Store Connect.

Before Contacting Support

Check whether monitoring is paused, whether the current app is listed in ignored apps, and whether the global shortcut conflicts with another Mac utility.

Privacy Reminder

Clipwell handles clipboard data. Do not paste passwords, secrets, private documents, or sensitive screenshots into support messages.

Common Fixes

Restart Clipwell from the menu bar, clear history if storage looks stale, and disable optional auto-paste if macOS permission prompts are blocking your workflow.
