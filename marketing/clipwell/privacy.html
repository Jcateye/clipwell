Clipwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Company

Clipwell is provided by MioMiao Labs LLC, operated by 深圳弥傲科技有限公司.

Summary

Clipwell is designed as a local-first Mac clipboard utility. Clipboard history is stored on your Mac so you can search, preview, and restore recent clips.

Data Collection

Clipwell does not require an account and does not upload clipboard contents to a Clipwell server. The current planned App Store version does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Local Clipboard History

When clipboard monitoring is enabled, Clipwell reads the macOS pasteboard and stores supported clipboard items locally on your device. You can pause monitoring, ignore selected apps, limit history length, and clear history from the app.

Support

If you contact support, you may choose to share diagnostic details. Do not send sensitive clipboard contents in support requests.

Changes

This policy may be updated before public release. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
